Architecture & NAT Traversal

Signaling vs. Data: Render only handles the lightweight room-handshake signaling. Once peers pair, 100% of the byte transfer moves directly device-to-device through a direct WebRTC DataChannel.

Symmetric NAT & Strict Firewalls: The app queries Google and Cloudflare STUN servers. If both sender and receiver are on restrictive corporate firewalls or carrier-grade symmetric NATs, a direct socket may time out without a TURN relay.

Direct-to-Disk Pipelining: Uses the Chromium File System Access API with batched 2MB buffers to bypass browser RAM bottlenecks completely.
