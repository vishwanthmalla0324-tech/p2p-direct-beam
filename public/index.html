Multi-Device Mesh Architecture

Multi-Device Mesh: Direct Beam establishes individual DTLS-encrypted DataChannels to every connected device. The file is sliced from the sender's disk and broadcast simultaneously to all receiving peers without touching our servers.

Bandwidth Scalability: Sender upload bandwidth is shared evenly across all connected peers. Receivers download at peak local peer speeds.

Zero RAM Footprint: Files of any size (50 GB+) are streamed in 64KB slices with 2MB chunked writing directly to the target system's filesystem.
