The Vision

Privacy is not a feature. It is the architecture.

From low-level microcontrollers and SPI communication to WebRTC peer-to-peer streaming, I engineer systems that eliminate middlemen. Whether routing encrypted payloads across distributed browsers or prototyping hardware logic on ESP32 silicon, the objective is uncompromising: sovereign, blazing-fast, decentralized software.

  • 01

    Peer-to-peer over centralized clouds

    Data shouldn't transit a third-party server when two nodes can communicate directly. Zero latency leaks, zero surveillance.

  • 02

    DTLS & cryptographic trust

    Security isn't bolted on afterwards. Every packet is signed, verified, and shielded by modern DTLS 1.3 cryptographic standards.

  • 03

    Silicon to browser fluency

    True systems mastery bridges hardware and the web: from C and ESP32 firmware to TypeScript and modern reactive interfaces.

  • 04

    Open-source velocity

    Ship real solutions to real problems. Direct Beam was engineered to make secure file transfer effortless, transparent, and accessible to all.
